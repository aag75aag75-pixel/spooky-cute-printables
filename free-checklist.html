🎃 FREE Halloween Party Checklist

Your 2-Week Plan for a Fun, Easy, No-Stress Kids' Party • Ages 4–10

📅 2 Weeks Before

  • Pick party date & time (60–90 minutes is perfect)
  • Make the guest list (8–12 kids = sweet spot)
  • Send invitations (page 17 of the Party Pack!)
  • Choose your 4–6 favorite games

🖨️ 1 Week Before

  • Print game pages (cardstock for cards & cut-outs)
  • Cut out cards, tags & props (movie night + scissors = done)
  • Shop the supplies list (right side →)
  • Prep small prizes or candy bags

⭐ 2–3 Days Before

  • Print 1 Party Passport per child (page 14)
  • Assemble treat bags with tags (page 16)
  • Set up the music playlist
  • Charge your phone for photos

🎉 Party Day

  • Clear floor space & set up 4–6 numbered stations
  • Put out snacks & drinks
  • Tape the witch poster on the wall (page 5)
  • Props basket near the entry — music on — have fun!

🍪 Easy Snack Ideas

  • "Witch fingers" — breadsticks
  • "Ghost bananas" — banana halves + chocolate chip eyes
  • "Pumpkin oranges" — clementines with green stems
  • Candy corn cups + juice boxes

🎵 Music

  • Search "Halloween songs for kids" — silly & upbeat. Any song works for Freeze Dance!

🆘 If Chaos Happens

  • Kids too wild? → Freeze Dance, then a slow round
  • Game flopped? → Switch to Scavenger Hunt (always works)
  • Out of candy? → Stickers + passport stamps as prizes
  • Stuck inside? → Every game works indoors
